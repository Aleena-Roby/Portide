Defect Resolution Summary (DEFECT-P4-001)

[RESOLVED] DEFECT-P4-001: Missing Shipping Company Binding in Vessel Arrival Request Details Modal

Identified during initial Phase 4 execution: In PortOperationsDashboard.tsx:1699, the modal attempted to render selectedRequest.shipping_company?.shipping_company_name. Because the Django REST serializer nests company info under selectedRequest.vessel?.company_name, the modal rendered "N/A" instead of "Pacific".

Targeted Fix Applied: Updated line 1699 to read: {selectedRequest.shipping_company?.shipping_company_name || selectedRequest.vessel?.company_name || 'N/A'}.

Retest Result: ARRIVAL-012 was re-executed against the live application. Confirmed company name renders as "Pacific" along with all vessel specifications, timestamps, and cargo parameters. Screenshot: ARRIVAL-012-PASS.png. Status: RESOLVED (PASS).
